Description

Earty life and education

Eaindra Kyaw Zin was born to a well-to-do family in Yangon , the youngest child of Mya Thida and Kyaw Zin. She is a granddaughter of Bo Zeya, one of the Thirty Comrades who founded the modern Burmese Army, a niece of Dagon Taya, a writer, and a first cousin of singer Hayma Ne Win and singer and film star Yaza Ne Win .Eaindra Kyaw Zin graduated from Yangon's Dagon 1 High School. She holds a BSc degree in chemistry.

Career

Eaindra Kyaw Zin did not originally plan to become an actress. She wanted to become an independent artist or a doctor. However, she entered the entertainment industry after winning the Miss Kokkine and Miss Christmas contests in 1996. After that, she began acting in TV series, including Loving Editor and Ah Hnine Mae, where she became popular as Po Tay. In 2000, she made her film debut as a leading actress in Pyaw Lai Kya Ya Aung. She won the Myanmar Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress in 2004 for her role in Flirtatious Sky. In 2017, she won another Myanmar Academy Award for her leading role in Knife in the Heart. Besides acting, she works as a model for advertisements. She also serves as the Vice-CEO of Pyay Ti Oo Education Foundation and the Chairperson of Thudra Film Production and The Bridge Myanmar.
